ABOUT ALEKYA

A life in color.

I'm Dr. Alekya Reddy, an orthodontist and a mostly self-taught artist. Art has been my companion since childhood—a space to slow down, follow my curiosity and turn everyday feelings into color.

Travel, photography and quiet moments inspire my work. I explore culture, nature and imagination through different mediums and textures, letting each painting find its own voice.

I hope these paintings offer a little calm, a spark of wonder or a moment that feels like your own.

With love,Alekya Reddy
