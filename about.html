ABOUT ALEKYA

Painting is how I slow time down.

I didn’t choose a single path in life; instead, I let my career and my creativity grow side by side. By day, I practice as an orthodontist, Dr. Alekya Reddy. My profession centers on precision, strict clinical perfection, and the careful aesthetics of crafting smiles—a job that I find incredibly rewarding. But when I step away from the clinic, I leave that rigid structure behind to explore a completely different kind of creation

Even as a child, drawing and painting made me happy. It was my favorite hobby, the thing I naturally came back to whenever I had the chance. Art and sketching have always been the quiet companions traveling right beside me through all of life’s highs and lows. While my dental career demands exact smile corrections and structured outcomes, the canvas offers me total liberation. It is my ultimate judgment-free zone—a quiet space where I don't have to worry about being perfect. Here, I can truly let go, transferring raw emotions, thoughts, and unspoken words into color and texture. Where medicine heals the body, art heals my soul.

I consider myself mostly self-taught. I don’t box myself into one fixed artistic style; instead, I let my work be guided entirely by intuition, curiosity, and constant experimentation with different mediums and textures.

Beyond the art, my world is fueled by travel and photography—capturing fleeting moments through my lens constantly fills my creative well and inspires each new collection. Exploring new places and capturing fleeting moments through my camera lens keeps me inspired. Eventually, those sights, textures, and memories find their way into my studio and onto the canvas. Sharing this process has brought me immense happiness and confidence, proving to me that I am doing what I truly love as I continue to learn and grow. Each collection I create is inspired by what I like, what I see, what I feel, and what I am interested in at that moment. For me, every canvas is just an experiment and a chance to make something out of nothing.

I am still learning, growing, and discovering who I am as an artist. It keeps me curious, inspired, and connected to what I love. I hope when you look at my work, you find your own sense of calm or a quiet escape within the layers. Thank you so much for being here and sharing this creative side of my life with me. It truly means a lot to have you as part of this journey

With love, Alekya Reddy
