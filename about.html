ABOUT ALEKYA

A life in color.

I'm Dr. Alekya Reddy, an orthodontist and a mostly self-taught artist. I've let my career and creativity grow side by side. Drawing and painting have been part of my life since childhood, and art remains a quiet companion through every high and low.

My work as an orthodontist centers on precision and the careful aesthetics of crafting smiles. Painting gives me the freedom to follow my feelings and curiosity. The canvas is my judgment-free space, where I can slow down, be honest and turn emotion into color and texture.

I enjoy exploring different mediums, textures and ways of telling a story. Culture, nature and imagination find their way into my paintings, often blending with the mood of the moment. I let intuition and experimentation guide the process, allowing each piece to develop its own character.

Travel and photography keep my creativity alive. A changing sky, an intricate temple, a quiet forest or a fleeting moment can become the beginning of a painting. The places I visit and the details I capture through my lens gradually find their way onto the canvas.

Every collection is shaped by what I'm seeing, feeling or curious about at the time. I'm still learning and growing as an artist, and that keeps this journey meaningful. I hope you find a little calm, a spark of wonder or a quiet escape in these layers. Thank you for being part of my story.

With love,Alekya Reddy
