Welcome

I'm so glad you're here.

I'm Alekya Reddy, a mostly self-taught artist based in India. This is a space for the places, memories and everyday moments that inspire me—translated into color and texture.

My paintings explore culture, nature and imagination. Travel and photography spark new ideas, while curiosity leads me to different mediums, styles and subjects.

Thank you for spending a little time with my art. I hope you find something here that feels familiar, brings you calm or simply makes you pause.

THE HEART OF MY WORK

Color, memory & feeling.

Art has been a companion since childhood. Alongside my life as an orthodontist, painting gives me room to slow down, experiment and express what words sometimes cannot.

My work grows from the things I see and feel: the beauty of nature, the richness of culture and the freedom of imagination. Each piece carries a little of that journey.
