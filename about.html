ABOUT ALEKYA

Painting is how I slow time down.

I didn't choose a single path in life — I let my career and my creativity grow side by side.

By day, I practice as an orthodontist, Dr. Alekya Reddy. My work centers on precision, clinical perfection, and the careful aesthetics of crafting smiles — work I find genuinely rewarding. But when I step away from the clinic, I leave that structure behind for something else entirely.

Drawing and painting made me happy even as a child; it was the one thing I always came back to. Art has been a quiet companion through every high and low since. Where my dental career demands exact corrections and structured outcomes, the canvas offers total liberation. It is my judgment-free zone, where I don't have to be perfect—just honest. Here, I let go, turning raw emotion into color and texture. Where medicine heals the body, art heals my soul.

As a mostly self-taught artist, I don't box myself into one fixed style. Instead, I let intuition, curiosity, and constant experimentation with mediums and textures guide my work.

Travel and photography fuel most of this creativity. Capturing fleeting moments through my lens keeps my creative well full, and those sights, textures, and memories eventually find their way onto the canvas. Each collection is shaped by whatever I'm feeling, seeing, or curious about at the time. Every piece is really just an experiment—a chance to make something out of nothing.

I'm still learning who I am as an artist, and that keeps me curious and connected to what I love. I hope you find a little calm, or a quiet escape, somewhere in these layers. Thank you for being part of this journey.

With love,

Alekya Reddy
